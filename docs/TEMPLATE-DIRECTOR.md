# Reusable Website Template — Director Playbook

## Responsibility
Own product quality, commercial integrity, accessibility, performance, SEO foundations, deployment safety, and post-commit reporting for the fictional Northstar driving-school demo and its future buyer adaptations.

## Priority order
1. Trust: no fabricated claims, broken contact links, or public pricing.
2. Conversion: understandable service categories and a verified enquiry destination.
3. Mobile UX: readable type, tappable controls, no overflow, efficient imagery.
4. Accessibility: keyboard support, visible focus, semantic structure, contrast, reduced motion.
5. Reliability: repeatable checks and verified deployment.
6. Discoverability: metadata grounded in confirmed facts.
7. Reusability: explicit localization steps for buyers in other countries.

## Release scorecard (0–5)
Conversion clarity; visual consistency/responsive UX; accessibility; performance/assets; SEO; security/privacy; automated verification; deployment reliability. Every score requires evidence. Unknown is not a pass. A broken CTA, exposed price, unverified business claim, missing asset, stale former-client identity, or failing deployment blocks release.

## Change workflow
Inspect main and preserve unrelated work; make scoped changes directly on main; run dependency-free checks; review changed files and workflow permissions; inspect the corresponding CI/deployment result; report changed files, actual checks, known risks and the next concrete action. No working branches or pull requests for routine iterations.

## Demo and buyer rules
- Northstar is fictional demonstration branding, not a verified business.
- No real client contact details, pricing, personal data or private business claims may remain in the current template.
- Demo calls-to-action must not imitate a functioning phone/WhatsApp channel; connect buyer-owned destinations only after approval.
- The South African locale is the starting example. International buyers need jurisdiction-specific category, language, currency, service-area and privacy/terms updates.
- Use stock imagery transparently. Never present a stock model or car as the buyer's real instructor, learner or fleet.

## Owner confirmations before launch
Buyer identity and brand approval; verified service area; contact destinations; current inclusions/pricing; lesson arrangements; image permissions; market-specific legal content; and deployment/domain ownership.
