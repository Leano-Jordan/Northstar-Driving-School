✳ GOOD TO KNOW

Before you
make a move.

A few useful things to have in mind before you get in touch.

Which training options can I ask about?+

Options may include learner support, practical lessons, test preparation, refresher training and specialist routes. Tell Northstar your goal so the relevant service can be confirmed.

Can I see prices before booking?+

Where the school publishes rates, compare them with lesson length and package inclusions. For tailored training, request an itemised quote and confirm any separate application or testing fees.

What should I include in my message?+

Mention your experience level, the outcome you want and whether you need theory support, practical lessons, test preparation or a tailored course. That helps make the conversation more useful.
